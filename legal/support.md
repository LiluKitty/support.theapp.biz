## Contact Support

If you need help with any of our apps, you can always reach us here:

**Email:** support@theapp.biz

We usually reply within **24–48 hours**.

To help us resolve issues faster, please include:

- The app name  
- A short description of the issue  
- Steps to reproduce (if possible)  
- Your device model (example: iPhone 15)  
- iOS version  
- App version

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which apps do you support?
We provide support for all applications published under **Theapp.biz**, including background editing apps, entertaiment tools, and others.

### Do your apps work offline?
Most of our apps process data directly on your device to protect your privacy.  
If an app requires an online feature, it will clearly state so inside the app.

### I found a bug. What should I do?
Please email **support@theapp.biz** with:

- Screenshot or screen recording  
- Description of the problem  
- Expected vs actual behavior  
- Device and iOS version  

### How do I request a feature?
Send us an email with your idea.  
We carefully review all feature requests and often implement the most helpful ones in future updates.
